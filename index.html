What it does

Everything your server needs

SpyraZ brings a full economy, invite tracking, role shop, and Blox Fruits marketplace all in one bot.

💬

Chat to Earn

Members earn 3 coins per message with a 30s cooldown and a 600 coin/day cap. No spam farming.

📨

Invite Rewards

Invite someone → they send 10 messages + stay 1 hour → you earn +50 coins. If they leave, −50. Fair system.

🛒

Role Shop

7 tiered roles from Rookie Spy to Director. Buy them with coins, get real Discord permissions. Roles auto-created on startup.

🍎

Blox Fruits Market

Buy any Blox Fruit with your Spy Coins. 40+ fruits with autocomplete search. Admin delivers in-game.

🎟️

Redeem Codes

Redeem special codes for 1,111 coins each. Great for giveaways. Each code is one-time per account.

🎁

Gift System

Send coins to any member with an optional message. DM notification included. Track how much you've gifted.

🏆

Leaderboard

Top 10 richest members displayed with medals. See your rank at the bottom even if you're not in the top 10.

📹

Video Requests

Members can request to appear in Abhay's videos. Requests post automatically to your set channel.

🆔

Profile Cards

Every member gets a profile with bio, roles, coin balance, and a special "Text from own" field for those with access.

How coins work

Economy Rules

📜 Earning Rules

  • 3 coins per message sent
  • 30 second cooldown between earning messages
  • Maximum 600 coins earnable per day via chat
  • +50 coins when your invite qualifies (10 msgs + 1 hr)
  • −50 coins if your qualified invitee leaves
  • +1,111 coins per redeemed code (one-time each)
  • Receive coins as gifts from other members
