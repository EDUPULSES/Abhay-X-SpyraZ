What it does

Everything in one bot

SpyraZ brings a full economy, invite tracking, role shop, and Blox Fruits marketplace — all in one personal bot.

💬

Chat to Earn

3 coins per message · 30s cooldown · 600 coin/day cap. No spam farming.

📨

Invite Rewards

+50 coins when your invite qualifies (10 msgs + 1 hr). −50 if they leave after.

🛒

Role Shop

7 tiered roles from Rookie Spy to Director. Real Discord permissions included.

🍎

Blox Fruits Market

Buy any Blox Fruit with Spy Coins. 40+ fruits with autocomplete. Admin delivers in-game.

🎟️

Redeem Codes

Special codes worth 1,111 coins each. One-time per account. Great for giveaways.

🏆

Leaderboard

Top 10 richest members with medals. See your rank even outside the top 10.

🎁

Gift System

Send coins to any member with an optional message. DM notification included.

📹

Video Requests

Members request to appear in Abhay's videos. Auto-posts to the set channel.

🆔

Profile Cards

Bio, coins, roles, and a "Text from own" field for those with owner access.
